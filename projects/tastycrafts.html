Tastycrafts

Bakery

Services

Brand Architecture / B2B Packaging / Confectionery Design

Overview

Tastycrafts needed a playful yet professional B2B edible decorations brand identity and retail packaging architecture instantly understood by bakers, pastry chefs, and home artists worldwide. Buttercup engineered the iconic crown-and-cake identity under the tagline 'Make the Edible, Incredible', structuring a vibrant multi-category packaging range spanning Pearl Sprinkles, Dazzling Glo, Candy Color, Luster Dust, and Sugar Paste.
