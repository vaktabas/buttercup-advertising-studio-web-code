# Buttercup Advertising Studio

Welcome to **Buttercup Advertising Studio (BAS)** — an award-winning creative branding, design, and advertising agency based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

## About the Studio
Founded and led by Creative Director **Sneha Vakta Shah**, we craft bold brand identities, strategic narratives, packaging design, and modern interactive digital experiences for ambitious businesses across India and worldwide.

## Manifesto
We walk the whole journey with a brand — often from the first word of its name to the creative that introduces it to the market. Along the way, every brand meets trouble: market disruption, copycats, inconsistent communication. We do everything in our power to keep the brand on track with experience from many categories, B2B and B2C. Just bold ideas.
